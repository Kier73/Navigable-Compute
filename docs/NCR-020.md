## Route language

Interpreter.realize(q,strategy=...) accepts a finite supplied permutation catalogue. Baseline retains NCR-019 order. Witness-first begins with the admitted ordinal witness attempt; span-first (Cube only) begins with the restricted equation span; exact-first begins with exact refinement. The remaining stages remain available after UNKNOWN or explicit budget refusal. Each semantic output still goes through the actual NCR-019 checker. A successful shorter rewrite can precede residual formation, but exact-first does not repeat a failed exact stage after rewriting. Different schedules under restricted budgets can therefore have different coverage; no optimality or identical bounded completeness claim.

The feature vector contains intention type, size bucket, overlap with support of current admitted rules, rule-count bucket, source width, and all exact per-route budgets. It excludes answers, source identities, timings, and the particular variable labels. Context calculation itself costs time and is charged in evaluation. No probe results are used as features.

## Paired training and policy

For each supplied training intention execute every offered full schedule under the same limits, rotate order across episodes, independently verify resolved outputs and refuse semantic disagreements. Measure synchronous full realize() including its semantic checker. Separate extra training audit occurs outside the measured span but inside total training time. Discard the entire paired timing episode if any duration is missing or regressed; unresolved outcomes count as unresolved, not as successful predictions. The native no-dependency path uses perf_counter_ns; the IFS path uses its actual recorder and validates each span. IFS's separate descriptive profile report remains policy-effect none; the NCR-020 adapter consumes the observed durations and checked resolution counts, not a claim of authority from the report.

For context c, strategy s retain (n, h, T), where n is paired episode count, h is verified resolved count, T is sum of measured wall ns. At least three observations and complete all-strategy coverage with h=n are required for learned choice. Minimize T/n with deterministic catalogue tie break. Otherwise baseline. This is empirical arithmetic mean on a small training sample, not a global optimum or prediction guarantee. Training charges every alternative, including losers. Actor and clock segment bind the policy; another segment defaults to baseline. A portable canonical packet retains only advisory statistics; exact validation checks paired coverage and numeric bounds, not authenticity of timing or source history. A forged yet structurally valid policy can degrade scheduling but cannot authorize a false result.

## Campaign

36 training intentions (six shapes repeated six times), with all offered strategies measured. Distinct held-out coordinate placements and source labels: 72 matched and 72 shifted intentions. Shifted requests include witness-hostile signed assignments and a changed overlap context, exercising both misprediction and default behavior. Compare frozen learned vs baseline route on each held-out intention, alternating measurement order, independently reauditing both results outside the measured times. No held-out feedback. Timing noise and workload dependence are expected; do not assert a minimum speedup. The synthetic workload is intentionally shaped to exercise different mechanisms and does not represent all computation. The benchmark reports training and policy costs separately from evaluation; no amortization claim without enough repetitions.

The dedicated Linux/Windows Python3.13 workflow uses actual IFS span and profile functions, reconstructs the report from original source spans and uploads span/profile/campaign JSON artifacts. The profiles describe full schedule calls, not isolated mathematical operations. Neither hashes nor timing establish remote attestation.
