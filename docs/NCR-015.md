# NCR-015 — Induced polynomial procedures on evidence-conditioned Grounds

Extends [NCR-014](NCR-014.md) and the [PGI-002](https://github.com/Kier73/In-Formation-Space/blob/b452125471b13ec20c5683d60d29f9ed115e77b7/experiments/ground-intelligence/PGI-002.md) distinction between empirical discovery and qualified callable semantics. Instead of storing answers for each target, learn a reusable vanishing polynomial and instantiate it in untrained multiplicative contexts. The primitive algebra and proposal grammar are supplied; learning discovers which candidate relations hold on a particular admitted fibre.

## Algebra

For Boolean coordinates, encode a monomial by its support mask m; multiplication is bitwise OR because x_i^2=x_i. Addition is XOR of terms. A qualified relation r vanishes on the current fibre F_h. For any monomial mask k, x^k r also vanishes; any finite XOR of such multiples vanishes. Thus a certificate

    q XOR residual = XOR_i (x^k_i r_i)

establishes that q and residual agree everywhere on F_h. If the residual is 0 or 1 the intention is resolved. Otherwise return UNKNOWN: irreducibility in this rewrite grammar is not proof of nonconstancy. This is a sound partial reduction system, not a complete Boolean ideal algorithm or a proof of polynomial-time general SAT.

For a rule with greatest integer mask p, rewrite any term m containing p by XOR of (m XOR p)*r. The multiplier is disjoint from p, and the highest differing bit between p and every lower term of r is present in p and absent from that multiplier. All replacement masks are therefore strictly below m, even after idempotent Boolean multiplication. Finite descent holds, but the number of steps can be exponential and terms can expand. Explicit step/search/term budgets refuse. The producer picks largest reducible mask and first admitted rule; the independent verifier uses Counter parity of the entire algebraic identity, not the same greedy rewriting. Work counts producer rule probes and emitted monomial visits, not every CPU operation or the independent audit.

## Admission and induction

The initial rule set comprises the checked parent equations and independently checked observation overlay equations. The overlay must be nonempty. From each observed polynomial with support at most eight variables, the grammar proposes singleton bits, pair XOR, pair AND, pair OR, at most 64 proposals total. Exact NCR-011 refinement plus independent verification establishes whether each proposal is constant. Only new vanishing rows enter the learned vocabulary (capacity 64). Negative/unknown candidates do not authorize a rule. This is exact cold mathematical feedback, not additional physical measurement. The grammar uses only observation support, not held-out targets.

A vocabulary is tied to source equations, observed equations and caller-owned context. Vocabulary.from_memory uses real NCR-014 receiver-admitted VGI history and binds the SHA-256 of its canonical snapshot. Packet restoration requires the same independently admitted fibre/context, cold rechecks each learned rule and compares canonical bytes. It stores only learned targets and values, not authority or all proof DAGs. It is not itself an E-CPU quorum-sealed artifact; the prior observation memory can be recovered from its native root separately. Warm reduction verifies algebraic certificates against locally admitted immutable rules, without rebuilding residual DAGs. This is a trusted in-process object discipline, not adversarial Python isolation.

## Discriminating test

The 128-bit parent is free, with observations parity(x0,x1)=0 and majority(x2,x3,x4)=1. The conditioned fibre has 2^126 members. Majority=1 implies each pair OR of its three variables equals 1. Training discovers these relations by the declared grammar. The 123 other coordinates are never used in training. Held-out queries combine randomly chosen monomial multiples of the discovered pair-OR identities across those spectator coordinates, with a constant offset. All are checked against actual cold exact refinement. This is deliberately a structured workload matched to the learned relations, not representative of arbitrary ANFs. The shifted workload adds an independent spectator bit and checks that unresolved targets stay unresolved; small random sources/targets are separately checked against exhaustive state enumeration.

Compare the source/evidence-only rewrite grammar, learned rewrite grammar and cold exact count. An algebraic solver with a stronger saturation or Boolean ideal basis could discover these relations directly; no claim that training is necessary or universally faster than other symbolic algorithms. Measure the full charged induction, warm independent verification, cold parent audits and restoration separately. The 128-bit count is implicit; the experiment does not enumerate it or claim arbitrary 128-bit computation at warm speed.
