## Intention contracts and route selection

Truth(q) asks whether a canonical ANF is zero everywhere, one everywhere, or MIXED on the current nonempty admitted vocabulary Ground. Cube(A,B) asks existence of a member with A=1,B=0, using disjoint masks. The result is POSSIBLE or EXCLUDED. A bounded unsuccessful route returns UNKNOWN (not a semantic certificate). The observer does not select an operation. Limits are explicit per mechanism. The current route order is a deterministic shape-based heuristic, not a learned or globally cost-optimal schedule.

Truth: checked rewrite -> two opposing source-address witnesses -> exact refinement of a strictly shorter verified residual, if available, otherwise exact refinement of original target. If rewriting exhausts its budget, the original target is retained. If the shorter residual route exhausts its budget, this implementation refuses UNKNOWN rather than paying for another exact attempt.

Cube: one-rule signed cofactor -> joint cofactor span -> source-address witness -> exact coordinate intersection. Each successful result independently verifies against the same receiver-admitted Ground and exact current rule catalogue digest. Each attempted route records produced/unknown/budget and elapsed local time, but the semantic verifier neither authenticates telemetry nor proves that the selected route was optimal. Budget exceptions alone permit escalation; invalid inputs or contradictory certificates refuse.

Address witnesses use at most five deterministic admitted overlay ordinals (first, last, midpoint, quartiles), never statistical inference. To establish mixed truth, produce two members with opposite values. To establish possible cube, produce one matching member. The independent checker verifies source/observation equations directly and checks the inverse ordinal. Failure to sample a witness proves nothing. No source sensing or external acquisition occurs.

## New composition: restricted equation span

For each admitted vanishing polynomial r_i restrict it to the requested cube C; write s_i=r_i|C. If a subset I has XOR_{i in I} s_i=1, then the cube is excluded: any Ground member in C would make each s_i=0. A sparse GF(2) elimination over monomial coordinates finds constant one in their linear span, tracking original rule indices by XOR provenance. The independent verifier uses occurrence parity of the selected original terms after simultaneous coordinate projection. This is complete for the linear span when bounded construction completes, not for the Boolean ideal or arbitrary nonlinear conjunction.

Example: x XOR y=0 and y XOR z=0. Intention x=1,z=0. Neither equation alone has constant-one cofactor; restricted rows are 1 XOR y and y. Their sum is 1. The new certificate contains just the indices of those two source rules. Counterexample: x XOR y=0 and 1 XOR yz=0, with x=0. The restricted rows y and 1 XOR yz cannot linearly combine to 1, but joint residual construction proves exclusion.

The span stage caps term/elimination charges at 1e6 and intermediate row size at 16384. These are not CPU instruction bounds; hashing, maximum-pivot selection, sorting, integer arithmetic and verification also cost time.

## Verified composition: rewrite then refine

A checked algebraic identity q XOR s= sum_j m_j*r_j establishes pointwise equivalence of q and s on the current Ground. When s has fewer terms, refine the original Ground conditioned on s=1, verify its exact count, and classify q from the same count. No target expansion or repeated rewrite is needed. Receipt carries both independent proofs. No global cost dominance follows merely from fewer terms.

## Test campaign

Tests exercise every route, forged proof, wrong source, changed request, budget exhaustion, unsupported inputs and seeded exhaustive small-state comparisons. The 128-bit campaign compares 112 requests across five source shapes to fresh exact native refinement: disjoint-pair cofactors, joint span, learned rewrite, composed rewrite+residual, opposite address witnesses, feasible residual and nonlinear joint fallback. Training/skill formation, Ground admission, portfolio cost including its own verification, separate reaudit and cold baseline are reported separately. No claim about universal speedup; direct exact comparison includes repeated parent admission and proof verification. No additional physical observations, quorum sealing or live E-CPU dispatch are performed. Those remain separate admission/placement operations.
