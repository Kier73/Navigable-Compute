# NCR-006 — Symbolic observer quotient before source-state enumeration

**Status:** finite exact constructive mathematics. **Date:** November 21, 2026. **Implementation:** navigable_compute/symbolic_quotient.py, symbolic_quotient_verify.py, symbolic_quotient_wire.py; tests/test_symbolic_quotient.py; symbolic_quotient_demo.py.

## The research question

NCR-004 showed that an observer's complete finite program language can be derived by minimizing the **reachable explicit source states first**, rather than constructing an enormous source transformation monoid. NCR-005 then generated exact character/one-hot coordinates for that observer-derived language. Both depended on explicit source states.

**NCR-006 asks whether the *state quotient itself* can be constructed without enumerating source states.**

Answer for a nontrivial, rigorously specified family: **yes**. Binary affine source operators and linear observer projections admit an exact, symbolic, future-stable, dimension-minimal quotient constructed solely from finite-field matrix relations. The method does **not** enumerate the 2^n source states, close the source's full operation monoid, or sample a trajectory.

## Exact source and observer contract

The fully declared stationary source is

\[
X=\mathbb F_2^n,\quad
T_a(x)=A_ax\oplus b_a,\quad
O(x)=Hx
\]

for n between 1 and 256, with 1–16 total affine generators and 1–32 Boolean observer rows. All matrices and biases are exact bitsets; serialized bitsets use canonical decimal strings. **All 2^n source states** are in scope, not just a few sampled or initially visited states.

A word w of allowed actions induces an affine source transformation. We ask for an exact quotient q such that

\[
q(x)=q(y)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\forall w\in A^*,\ O(T_w(x))=O(T_w(y)).
\]

This is future observational equivalence for **all finite action words** under the *stipulated* source equations; it does not certify an unknown environment or a changing law. Affine biases cancel when comparing x,y:

\[
O(T_w(x))\oplus O(T_w(y))=HA_w(x\oplus y).
\]

Consequently, the observer-relevant dual subspace is

\[
W=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb F_2}\{hA_w:
h\text{ a row of }H,\ w\in A^*\}.
\]

The constructor starts with the rows of H and repeatedly closes their span under right multiplication by each A_a. Matrix rows are represented as Python integer bitsets. It uses exact high-pivot Gaussian elimination; rank can grow at most n times. Let B be a canonical basis matrix for W with rank r.

The native quotient state is

\[
z=q(x)=Bx\in\mathbb F_2^r,
\]

and its operators are **constructed**, not provided:

\[
BA_a=M_aB,\quad c_a=Bb_a,
\qquad z'=M_az\oplus c_a.
\]

Since every row of H lies in W, an observer decoder D satisfies H=DB; the observed result is simply Dz.

**Theorem (exact future sufficiency and minimality).** Equality of Bx and By is equivalent to equality of every finite future observer output, since B spans exactly W. For any pair of states distinguished by B, there exists a word w and an observer row that distinguishes their future outputs. Therefore the quotient has exactly 2^r future-observation equivalence classes, and no deterministic future-correct quotient can have fewer classes. This is a minimality result for the defined observer contract, not the statement that the compiled affine representation minimizes arbitrary circuit size, verification work, or physical resources.

## Full-source independent verification

The producer's source, basis, induced operators, decoder, and distant result are **not authoritative because they are packaged with a digest**.

The independent checker uses a separate **low-pivot, frontier-based dual-span saturation**. It checks that the supplied r basis rows are independent, span exactly the independently obtained future-relevant row space, contain the observer rows, and satisfy each original source-to-quotient transition identity at the bitset level. It reconstructs observer readouts without calling the producer's decoder.

For a distant-time query, the verifier composes the source word and powers the **original n-bit affine matrices** independently. It projects the resulting exact source state with B and compares both the derived quotient state and the observed bits. The navigation and independent audit each use logarithmically many affine compositions, but at **different matrix dimensions**; neither iterates the time trajectory.

Separate canonical transport packets perform full-field validation, duplicate-key refusal, unknown-field refusal, canonical decimal parsing, digest comparison and source-bound independent semantic checks. They are mathematical integrity records, not signatures or E-CPU execution credentials.

## Measured synthetic experiment

The demonstration specifies 128 original state bits, including 125 hidden bits with nontrivial internal permutation and coupling. Three allowed source operations also toggle the visible bits, but the hidden subsystem cannot influence the visible observer. Consequently:

- Original state count: 2^128 (implicit, never enumerated).
- Generator matrices: exactly three explicitly specified 128-bit affine operators.
- Visible output: three bits.
- Symbolically derived quotient: **r=3 bits, eight distinguishable states**.
- Observer-native instruction family: three exact 3-bit affine generators, composed without original monoid closure.
- Requested logical coordinate: 10^100+173.
- Query method: binary powering of the generated three-bit affine operator.
- Verification: a separately written checker powers the original 128-bit operators and reproduces the output.

The number of operator products is logarithmic in the logical coordinate; the producer and verifier do not pretend that the original source matrices, law discovery, source evidence, memory or output costs vanish. The origin laws are supplied mathematical fixtures rather than measured physical dynamics. This is **not** a measured CPU acceleration relative to an optimized native implementation, and it does not demonstrate an unauthorized remote path as an operator.

### Deliberate counterexample

A 48-bit shift register whose first observable bit subsequently exposes every other source bit has **r=48**, even though the immediate observation contains just one bit.

An eight-bit quotient budget therefore **refuses** the claim. This is exactly what a sound future-aware representation constructor should do. A one-bit *present* observation does not imply one-bit future sufficiency.

## Relationship to the wider programme

NCR-006 is a symbolic **state** quotient and an exact *affine operator grammar* on that quotient. It does **not** compute the complete observer instruction monoid of NCR-004 for arbitrary large r. It also does not infer a non-affine symbolic interpretation, learn an unknown source matrix from observations, or prove any present hardware implements the synthesized law.

NCR-005's character diagonalization applies when the *resulting finite observer action group* meets its precise elementary-abelian requirements. NCR-006's affine quotient supports noncommuting, nonbijective and coupled actions without attempting to force character coordinates.

This creates a useful division:

1. **NCI-001:** generate fully finite transformations from stated geometric invariants.
2. **NCR-004:** exact future equivalence and observer language from explicit finite source generator maps.
3. **NCR-005:** exact operator representations of that finite observer-language monoid.
4. **NCR-006:** construct future equivalence and a native affine processor symbolically for an implicit binary-affine source of 2^n states, before source-state enumeration.

The next experiment should combine *symbolic* invariant synthesis, source-law evidence qualification, observer-language extraction, cost-aware representation selection, and VGI/E-CPU native admission. General nonlinear and neural dynamics require separate proofs, approximate error bounds, or explicit refusal. Reductions of output size alone do not establish smaller future-stable state spaces.

**Primary throughline:** rather than predict every hidden state and emulate all source operations, derive the mathematical distinctions an intention actually requires, construct an exact state and operator Ground preserving those distinctions, then bind the Ground to a separately qualified realizer.
