# Research frontier — from exact observer machines to constructed computational worlds

**November 20, 2026. This document distinguishes demonstrated capabilities from research hypotheses.**

## Central research challenge

Can a sufficiently precise intention be compiled into an *efficiently addressable and independently verifiable computational reality*—a representation, transition law, required observation, and physical realization plan—with less cumulative physical work than sequentially running a conventional machine to the same result?

This is an ambition about **constructive mathematical representation**. It does not assert all possible computations can be fast-forwarded; it does not claim RH implies P=NP. RH/GRH, including the November 20 Alpöge/Claude proof announcement, is a research **motivation**. The code does not import the claimed Lean theorem or depend on its correctness. The suggestive analogy is that a carefully selected chain of representations can expose constraints invisible to a single native representation. A hypothetical future RH-inspired arithmetic compiler needs explicit input-domain, map preservation, inverse transformation, proof and cost contracts to earn admission.

The user-supplied intuition, *“Reality is in-formation, and the stories we make; matter,”* is useful as motivation to ask how descriptions become externally checkable realizations. It is not a substitute for mathematical or physical evidence.

## New: description → possibility Ground → qualified processor instance

[NCP-001](NCP-001.md) introduces a separate contract-first construction lane. A
brief declares allowed transition effects, initial uncertainty, safety and
terminal goals; the compiler builds a finite relational Ground, proves the
existence of a guaranteed convergent control law, and indexes *all* locally
rank-optimal qualified processor laws through mixed-radix coordinates. It
never assumes the program exists beforehand. Nonrealizable briefs obtain a
closed adversarial-trap witness. A separate checker verifies source clauses,
Bellman ranks, source-bound selected instance identity, and refusals.

A 32-factor synthetic example addresses a 2^128-state Cartesian space by
checking 512 local states and navigating to a mathematically realized
controller at instance coordinate 10^30+17. This relies on **stipulated
factor independence**; it does not entail a cheap solution for coupled
machines or confirm externally realized actuator effects. Further work
should synthesize the possibility geometry itself from typed invariants,
link factors by certified interfaces, and bind constructed operations to
independently admitted E-CPU observer-host capabilities.

## Five discriminating next experiments

1. **Symbolic time-scoped quotient (NCQ-002 continuation):** NCQ-002 now demonstrates exact collapse to one task state after an overwrite, and to two states for a single distant high-bit query. The remaining frontier is to discover the same quotient *without enumerating the source state table*, via dependency slicing, constraint graphs, BDDs/SMT and proof-carrying representation synthesis.
2. **Symbolic coupling:** Move beyond static disconnected byte factors. Use reduced ordered BDDs, incremental SAT, AIGs, algebraic state space and bounded exact relational composition. Do not assert a compact quotient until closure and target preservation are certified. Record exploding diagrams, hard SAT instances and refusal as useful research outcomes.
3. **Intent-driven law search:** Admit additional general-purpose grammars, including true conditional branching and parameterized nonlinearity. Search by typed semantics/CEGIS and prove each *candidate* by conformance or complete finite checks, not by fit alone. Optimize law-description size, construction effort, external output equivalence, verifier cost, and porting cost.
4. **Independent-host certificates:** Define a canonical JSON/byte wire contract shared by E-CPU and VGI. Have a producer compile a Ground on one host and an independent checker reconstruct its proof on another with different language/algorithm. Preserve source identity and the distinction between logical coordinate and wall time.
5. **Physical and neural grounds:** Use E-CPU Model Host and VL-I causal layers for actual hidden-state observations. A candidate law first fits a chronological training block; validation and final test remain untouched. Numerical approximations must include precision, validity horizon, and explicit refusal for unsupported extrapolation. The TCN-003 phase-aware exact metaplectic path offers an alternative for circuits with quadratic structure; preserve global phase when interference requires it.

## Complexity / falsification ledger

The coarsest observer equivalence in a finite deterministic system exists, but finding it can require enumerating exponentially many concrete states. Some succinct transition systems encode PSPACE-hard reachability problems. There are established no-fast-forwarding results for broad Hamiltonian families and exact simulation does not in general yield a cheap coordinate address. General satisfiability is NP-complete; this repository has no implication for P versus NP.

A successful acceleration claim needs an accounting that includes law/representation discovery, Ground admission, independent verification, stored advice or cached state, target navigation, communication, materialization and accuracy. For arbitrarily long fixed-input outputs, even writing the requested output may dominate. A law chosen retrospectively from the answer is not a computational acceleration unless its construction cost and source of information are counted.

**Success would be a growing, evidence-backed portfolio of task-relative mathematical processors that can be constructed, transported, independently checked, and used across observer-hosts.** That is an ambitious, falsifiable research objective independent of whether any single conjecture supplies a universal technique.

## NCI-001 (implemented) — invariants generate the transition space

[NCI-001](NCI-001.md) changes the constructor's **input contract**, rather than
merely expanding its opcode set. It receives finite geometry and requirements:
metric locality, symmetry, preserved observable labels, safe/goal membership,
and admitted initial states. It generates legal one-step transformations from
these constraints, derives goal-directed ranks from the resulting quotient
relation, and computes a factored coordinate space of all shortest-progress
symmetry-equivariant processors. Its mathematical receipts include an
independently checked obstruction when the invariants make realization
impossible. Nothing in NCI-001 imports or proves RH.

**Research question NCI-002:** can the mathematical Ground and symmetry itself
be discovered from an intention and measured distinctions, with independent
validation of its geometric and semantic authority? **NCI-003:** admit
state-coupled constraints with symbolic relation construction and record when
symbolic structure grows more costly than execution. **NCI-004:** bind a selected
law to an authorized E-CPU realizer without confusing mathematical existence
with physical or operational availability.

## NCR-002 — Self-interpretation as a construction constraint (implemented)

[NCR-002](NCR-002.md) derives a four-primitive reflective basis from seven
required semantic operations in a finite recipe grammar, constructs a task-
Ground-specific program encoding, generates the evaluator AST from syntax
semantics, and tests actual double-level self-interpretation with a separate
continuation-stack machine. An exact factoradic index selects among 120
representational encoding gauges; it does not confer 120 semantic CPUs.

**Next:** search for language/representation grammars rather than fixed
macro recipes; qualify the generated evaluator through inductive operational
semantics in a formal proof kernel; couple code generation to authenticated
E-CPU host capabilities. Count all construction, validation, source transport,
physical interpretation and result rendering costs. Unknown or intractable
candidate grammars should produce a refusal or conditional hypothesis, not
assertions of substrate-free computation or general speedups.


## NCR-003 — Observer-generated semantic language (implemented)

[NCR-003](NCR-003.md) constructs a task-relative language by quotienting the
NCS-001 operator monoid under all two-sided program contexts that preserve
selected outputs. This replaces the predeclared five-form grammar with a
**finite syntactic monoid** generated from behavior. Full-state observation
retains 29 opcodes on the 5×5 Ground, goal observation reduces to five; an
adversarial observer requires six classes, not four as one-sided inspection
incorrectly suggests. A Cayley regular action executes the resulting codes
on their own code states. This is exact finite algebraic self-application,
**not** NCR-002's full AST-level self-interpretation.

**Next:** obtain a certified quotient without fully enumerating the source
operator closure, using symbolic context constraints and counterexamples;
reconcile the quotient-language program with the generated reflective AST
interpreter; independently bind proof-carrying language certificates to E-CPU
authority and VGI Ground qualification. Compare total construction and
materialization costs against direct task execution.


**Language continuity:** NCR-003 additionally constructs exact one-way
surjective mappings from richer to coarser task-language quotients, with
independent proofs of composition preservation and explicit nonrefinement
witnesses when a proposed reverse map would fabricate erased semantics.
This is a mathematical continuity certificate, not a durable E-CPU process
identity, authenticated host observation, or migration of physical state.
