# NCR-017 — Induce a proof-producing operation from successful procedures

Extends [NCR-016](NCR-016.md). The question is whether previous successful proofs can determine a new procedure which generalizes to different mathematical source shapes, not just larger query coordinates. Related work: [PGI-002](https://github.com/Kier73/In-Formation-Space/blob/main/experiments/ground-intelligence/PGI-002.md) normalizes verified trace motifs and transfers callable semantics; [VL-I PFI-001](https://github.com/Kier73/VL-I/blob/00fd4026dc3c35bc130d2d09ab9d88b354616d7b/docs/PROBLEM_FORMED_INTERPRETER_PFI001.md) forms an interpreter from source structure. NCR-017 uses the actual NCR-016 learned proof records and NCR-015 verifier, rather than a duplicate proof kernel.

## The extracted motif

In the Boolean ring, a source equation r=1 XOR sum_{e in E} x^e = 0 describes parity-one monomial observations. A successful NCR-016 trace sometimes has the form g*r=g, where g=product_{i in S}(1 XOR x_i) is the indicator of all coordinates S being zero. Its ANF comprises every subset of S. The observation implies g=0 whenever S intersects every e in E: each nonconstant monomial contains a variable i whose factor x_i*(1 XOR x_i)=0. Therefore the new rule is the clause OR_{i in S} x_i=1.

A certificate consists of XOR of the original r multiplied by each monomial in g. Its exact identity is checked independently using the existing verifier. This remains valid for arbitrary monomial degree and coordinate relabeling, provided the condition is satisfied. Constant-one in r and a nonempty admitted source fibre are essential; a parity-zero observation does not authorize this rule.

## Induce and activate

induce((v1,v2,...)) scans successful actual NCR-016 Learned proof objects. It checks chronological earlier-rule indices, target equality, and the exact product identity, then recognizes the supplied motif by the full subset/downset shape and term intersection. At least two distinct source polynomials must support the operation; this tests diversity, not independent sampling. Skill stores these checked polynomial witnesses rather than any source-specific conclusion or target catalogue. Skill.restore checks every witness from canonical bytes; no physical observation authority is inferred. The generalization template and recognizer are provided by the implementation, not autonomously invented program syntax.

Activation converts the incoming admitted equation into a hypergraph E of term supports and enumerates its minimal hitting sets with arity <=k. Starting with {empty}, for each edge e retain each existing S intersecting e; otherwise branch into S union {i} for i in e, provided the arity cap permits. Deduplicate and remove strict supersets. Induction over edges shows every minimal hitting set of a prefix within the cap remains represented, since any final hitting set contains a minimal hitting set of each prefix and can extend it through the next edge. All terminal sets are inclusion-minimal within the bound. Independent small-domain tests compare every subset against direct intersection and one-element deletion criteria. The proof of each admitted output does not rely on completeness of search.

The number of minimal transversals may be exponential. Work bounds candidate generation and subset comparisons; intermediate candidates are capped at 4096, arity at 11 (2048 factor terms), retained rules at 64, aggregate emitted products at 1e6. Budget refusal is atomic for activation. Sorting, hashing, initial Ground qualification, skill check and independent proof verification also take time. The algorithm is a structural hypergraph transversal enumeration, not a general polynomial-time SAT algorithm or a claim of novel hypergraph mathematics. A mathematically stronger symbolic solver is an appropriate future comparator.

## Held-out shape controls

Train on two variable-disjoint majority-three sources, through the real NCR-016 symbolic induction. Restore the skill from its portable packet. Test on sources not used during training:
- Three disjoint triple monomials (nine coordinates): 27 minimal transversals of arity 3, none reachable by the prior grammar (support >8 and no triple proposals). Compare all 27 independently instantiated spectator-coordinate intentions with cold exact refinement, plus shifted unobserved-bit controls.
- A 48-coordinate star of pair monomials: with arity <=3, the sole minimal transversal is the common center. The other minimal transversal contains all 47 leaves and is intentionally outside the cap. No enumeration of the 48-coordinate assignments is needed to form the rule. Test exact cold equality on the new source.
- Opposite constant, forged motif, duplicate training source, zero budgets, and 160 seeded small hypergraphs checked against complete subset enumeration.

Both large fixtures have width 128; no 128-bit source-state enumeration is claimed. The benchmark separately charges training, skill induction/restoration, new Ground admission, rule activation, warm interpretation and fresh exact target formation. Learned rule packet restoration still uses NCR-015 cold requalification. Skill portability is across admitted mathematical sources, not live remote authentication or quorum recovery of the vocabulary itself.
