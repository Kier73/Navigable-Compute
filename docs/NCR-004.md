# NCR-004 — Construct an observer language before constructing its source processor algebra

**Status:** executable finite mathematical experiment, 20 November 2026. Standard-library Python, no model service. [Source](../navigable_compute/preclosure_language.py), [independent checker](../navigable_compute/preclosure_verify.py), [canonical wire](../navigable_compute/preclosure_wire.py), [deterministic demo](../preclosure_demo.py), and [tests](../tests/test_preclosure_language.py).

## Research question

NCR-003 constructed the entire monoid of source programs, then obtained an observer-relative syntactic quotient. Its core weakness is the expensive first operation: enumerating the source monoid may dwarf its task-relative quotient. VL-I TIME-001/002 now studies interpreters whose observational contracts change while a causal Ground remains active. Could we **derive the required language first**, and let qualified new observations refine that language without losing the validity of the earlier one?

NCR-004 does this for a finite deterministic, stationary set of source generator maps. It **does not enumerate the source monoid before quotienting**. A generator source may come directly from NCI-001's invariants and canonical law coordinates, avoiding NCS-001's closure stage entirely. A different declarative fixture can supply finite source generators directly; those are a stipulated mathematical hypothesis and not automatically a measured physical Ground.

## Two-stage equivalence theorem

Let `S` be a finite state set, `G = {g_1,...,g_k}` a set of total deterministic transformations on S, `B` a nonempty allowed initial set, and `O:S -> Y` the observer's exact labeling. Let `R` be the least subset containing `B` and closed under every `g`.

**State equivalence:** for `s,t` in R, define

    s ≈ t   iff   O(w(s)) = O(w(t))  for every finite generator word w.

Compute its coarsest partition by starting from `O` labels and repeatedly splitting any block whose generator successors fall into different blocks. The fixed point is an observation-preserving transition congruence: it defines a finite quotient `Q = R/≈` with exact generator actions on Q. The implementation checks reachability, cap limits, and every generator map.

**Program equivalence:** for arbitrary source words `u,v`, NCR-003's two-sided contextual equivalence is

    u ≡ v  iff  O(a u b(s)) = O(a v b(s))
                  for all source words a,b and all s in B.

Since `R = {b(s): b in G*, s in B}`, and `≈` compares the results under **all** trailing observation contexts `a`, we have

    u ≡ v  iff  [u(x)]≈ = [v(x)]≈  for all x in R.

Consequently, the monoid of transformations **generated on Q**, not on S, is isomorphic to the program quotient `G*/≡` sought by NCR-003. This is an exact finite theorem for the stated semantics. It is not approximate state embedding or an empirical model fit.

The constructor therefore only computes reachable states and Moore partition refinement on S, followed by operator closure on Q. The source monoid is never generated. Canonical shortest generator words identify resulting opcodes; the derived multiplication table and program powering are exact in the quotient.

## Separate independent checker

The checker does **not** call the producer's partition/closure functions. It:

1. Recomputes source reachability using repeated boolean fixed-point propagation rather than the producer's breadth-first traversal.
2. Computes state equivalence via **backwards pairwise distinguishability**: pairs with differing outputs are initially marked, and a predecessor pair becomes marked when any generator leads to a marked pair.
3. Rebuilds source-to-quotient generator maps and verifies exact observation preservation.
4. Computes canonical normal forms through priority-queue shortest-word search, not the producer's closure BFS.
5. Independently calculates each multiplication cell and checks source-bound canonical SHA-256.
6. Checks a language-revision homomorphism over every multiplication cell, every class and its source generators; recomputes the explicit non-invertibility witness.

An adversary who changes a quotient, map, normal form, cost profile, or migration receipt and **recomputes every digest** must still fail mathematical verification. Hashes are integrity markers under an externally trusted source reference, **not signatures, evidence-source identity, host authority or physically measured Ground truth**.

## Discriminating results

### A source operation space too large to enumerate for the intended task

Let S be two visible states multiplied by eight hidden positions. The source has three primitive generators: visible toggle; eight-cycle on hidden positions; hidden transposition `(0 1)`. The last two generate the full symmetric group S8; the visible toggle commutes with them. Therefore the stipulated source monoid has

    2 · 8! = 80,640 distinct transformations.

The independent **control oracle** does enumerate these once in the benchmark and test to check that count. **The production NCR-004 constructor never does.** With observations restricted to the visible bit, every hidden position is future-indistinguishable. Its processor has **2 state classes and 2 operations**. This is not a universal speedup: source generator maps are given, original state space has 16 entries, and the independent group census is separately charged as a validation oracle.

### Source invariant construction still works

For NCI-001's 3×3 / 5×5 / 7×7 geometric fixtures, the constructor obtains the **generator laws directly from invariants** without first constructing NCS-001's source monoid. Goal-observer quotient languages have **3 / 5 / 7 operations**, matching NCR-003's source-first reference calculation. Exact observers retain **4 / 29 / 38**, respectively; constant observers retain 1. A deliberately restricted 5×5 starting-state/label contract has **6 contextual operations**, not the invalid four-class one-sided quotient.

### Language continuity under new observations

A separate cyclic model has 16 source states, with visible toggle and an eight-cycle hidden coordinate. As the observer's admitted labeling changes from **visible state** to **visible + hidden parity** to **fully identified hidden position**, its derived language sizes become **2 -> 4 -> 16**. All three interpretations are grounded in **the same exact transformation maps and initial set**.

For a new label partition strictly refining the old label partition, the program quotient generated from the new labels admits a canonical surjective monoid homomorphism onto the old language:

    π(new_a · new_b) = π(new_a) · π(new_b)

and every new Moore state class maps to one old class. The certificate records both mappings, source dynamics identity, both distinct observation/language digests, and explicit pairs of new operations that the old observer could not distinguish. These pairs witness why a reverse reconstruction is generally impossible without retained source evidence.

**Changed dynamics are not a compatible language revision.** A different source generator order, transition map, initial set, or source identity is refused under the old dynamics digest. It must be treated as a **different Ground epoch**, retaining the old observation records as separately addressable evidence. This intentionally mirrors VL-I TIME-002's distinction between continuing causal identity and changed interpretation: NCR-004 checks mathematical continuity only; it does not itself maintain a live observation stream or independently authenticated causal ledger.

## Portable evidence and intended interoperation

`encode_preclosure` and `encode_revision` produce strictly canonical JSON bytes, capped at 2 MB. A receiver reconstructs the finite source declarations and verifies the entire semantic proof with the independent checker, including provenance *references*. Duplicate keys, extra fields, malformed nested arrays, altered values and source-mismatch packets are refused. There is no code execution during packet decoding.

**VL-I TIME-002 adapter proposal (not implemented here):** when TIME-002 accepts a genuinely observed environmental sample, its retained source identity, epoch, full-revision digest, and current candidate law should remain outside this mathematical packet. Once the source transition law is separately qualified, translate the admitted finite generator maps and observation labels into a `GeneratorObservation`; construct NCR-004's language and preserve a source-bound revision certificate. If a subsequent real sample contradicts that transition law, withdraw the language's use **for the new epoch**, keep old receipts for the earlier epoch, and require new admission. No offline quotient can prove an unobserved future environment will keep obeying its measured law.

**E-CPU / VGI adapter proposal (not implemented here):** bind the exact mathematical packet to existing signed receiver observations, semantic translation contracts, Hypervisor authorization and recovery-lineage authority; do not reinterpret local SHA-256 as authenticated host testimony or grant a constructed processor physical actuation by inference. Receiver-side resource capacity and fencing, including E-CPU NCB-006, remain separate admission conditions.

The deterministic demonstration reports original full-source group census (as an **independent control**), actual quotient sizes, language digests, portable packet sizes, valid and refused revisions, and an exact output after `10^100+173` repeated program steps. The complete suite also includes 70 seeded random finite four-state generator families checked against an independently enumerated full-source **two-sided contextual** equivalence oracle when within the source budget.

## Complexity and refusal

Let `S` be source state count, `k` primitive generators, `Q` observer state classes, and `L` distinct quotient operations. This reference constructor performs reachability in `O(kS)`, iterated Moore refinement of bounded finite state tables, and closure of at most `L` quotient maps with `O(kLQ)` tuple entries and `O(L²Q)` multiplication-table work. The independent verifier uses an `O(kS²)`-per-pass pair-distinguishability fixed point, plus quotient closure, canonical word checking and multiplication verification. The wire is bounded; large `S` or `L`, unbounded expression languages, approximate observations, partial/unknown transitions and unsupported law changes **refuse or require a different qualified Ground family**.

This does not remove physical host work, source-law acquisition, mathematical model selection, model interpretation or observation costs. An observer that requires full hidden state can force large `Q` and `L`; an arbitrary succinct source may remain intractable. No P=NP or RH-derived complexity assertion is made.
