# NCR-023 — Recursive coalescence and sustained fleet workload

NCR-022 coalesced proofs referenced to the immutable original source. NCR-023
permits a proof to depend on earlier independently qualified contributions by
content address, rather than local learned-rule ordinal. The mechanism is an
application of IFS CAG's causal anti-entropy to the NCR Boolean proof kernel.

## Admission and convergence

Each canonical packet pins the receiver-owned Ground and original catalogue,
a descriptive actor/task label, a canonical nontrivial vanishing ANF and a
sequence of terms (base index or dependency digest, monomial multiplier).
The exact identity is g = XOR_j m_j r_j. Dependency resolution is an iterative
topological traversal. Independently count projected monomial parity;
missing parents stay PENDING, false identities and descendants of INVALID
parents become INVALID, and only VERIFIED relations may be used. Cyclic or
otherwise unready descriptions remain pending rather than being qualified.
Malformed, cross-context or over-budget batches refuse atomically. Invalid
mathematical descriptions are retained as quarantine evidence, not authority.
Content-addressed set union converges within capacity, not consensus or
authentication. Canonical snapshots retain pending/invalid evidence and
reconstruct all states on recovery. Explicit budgets: 512 packets, 64 KiB
each, 16 MB total, 64 depth, 16M audited product-term visits per closure.
No liveness assertion under missing parents or capacity refusal.

Materialization uses actual NCR-015 Vocabulary and Reduction verifier again,
translating qualified dependency digests to deterministic earlier native
rule indices. Deduplication is by mathematical relation; at most 64 retained
native rules. No mathematical authority comes from an actor label or digest.

## Workload

A 128-bit Ground has 32 disjoint majority-three equations (96 source variables)
and 32 spectator coordinates. Eight independent spawned process slots receive
leaf tasks, then five balanced binary joining generations. Each worker
requalifies the complete ancestor closure for its join. Leaf proofs use
original product identities; joins XOR their two qualified relations.
The coordinator does not fabricate worker outputs. A fresh observer first
receives only the root (pending), then the other packets in reverse order,
duplicates, and restores the complete canonical inventory. The final requested
intention is a new monomial multiple of the root. Compare its one-reference
native proof against a direct flattened original-source certificate (128
base multipliers for 32 leaves), both independently verified. A small 4-leaf
campaign additionally uses exact residual counting. Large workloads use
exact polynomial parity, not exhaustive source enumeration. Changed
intention and forged-parent controls refuse.

The five-minute CI campaign repeats complete rounds with independently
shuffled source ordering and spectator assignments, fresh packet inventories,
a persistent eight-slot process pool, explicit requalification, late delivery
and restoration each round. It runs for at least 300 elapsed seconds (finishes
its current round; pool shutdown additional), with 9-minute job timeout.
Report includes rounds, actual distinct process IDs, max depth, proof packet
count, source and dependency description bytes, aggregate worker elapsed
time, p50/p95 round wall time and audit term visits. It does not claim a
single 300-second unbounded growing memory or distinct physical hosts.
Training/coordination/verification all take real time. Flattened certificates
are a strong direct baseline; recursive sharing is valuable when earlier
qualified facts are reused, not necessarily faster for every isolated proof.
